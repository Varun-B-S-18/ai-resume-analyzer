Problem:
Job seekers often struggle to understand whether their resume matches a particular job description. Manually comparing resumes with job requirements is time-consuming and may cause candidates to miss important skills or keywords required by recruiters and ATS systems.

Solution:
The AI Resume Analyzer analyzes a candidate's resume and compares it with a given job description using AI. It extracts relevant information such as skills, experience, education, and keywords, then provides a match score and identifies areas where the resume can be improved.
